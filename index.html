FOR NUTRITIONISTS & VETERINARIANS

Give Three Roots a professional look before you recommend a feed program.

Three Roots welcomes independent evaluation by animal nutritionists and veterinarians. We do not ask professionals to rely on marketing claims. We invite you to review the current label, intended use, ingredients, guaranteed analysis and the animal’s complete ration before deciding whether a Three Roots product is appropriate for a client.

01

Label transparency

The current Super Mix 9 Grains label identifies intended species, ingredients, guaranteed analysis and feeding guidance. That gives professionals a concrete starting point for evaluation.

02

Practical local access

Manufacturing in Weslaco and growing retail availability can help clients obtain the same product consistently without adding unnecessary sourcing complexity.

03

Value matters to compliance

A feeding program only works when the client can follow it consistently. Practical price and local availability can be relevant when professionals discuss realistic feeding options with families.

04

Direct manufacturer contact

Professionals can contact Three Roots directly to ask about current labels, product availability and intended use rather than relying only on second-hand retail information.

05

Multi-species conversations

Three Roots serves customers raising poultry, cattle, sheep, goats and swine. Product selection should always be matched to species, age, production stage, health status and the total ration.

06

Professional judgment comes first

Three Roots encourages nutritionists and veterinarians to recommend only what fits the individual animal and feeding objective. The company’s role is to provide clear product information and responsive local support.
